8. Key Design Decisions

DecisionRationale
log1p target transformIncome distribution is right-skewed; log scale normalises the target, improving GBR training stability and RMSE.
Gradient Boosting over Random ForestGBR achieves lower bias on tabular socio-economic data through sequential residual correction.
OrdinalEncoder for categoricalsPreserves ordinality of Good/Average/Poor categories; handles unknown values at inference via unknown_value=-1.
Median imputationRobust to outliers in skewed agricultural numeric features.
@st.cache_data on data loadsPrevents re-reading 47,970-row CSV on every Streamlit interaction.
joblib persistenceEntire sklearn Pipeline (preprocessor + model) serialised as one object; eliminates feature-order bugs at inference.

9. Summary

The Farmer Income Predictor is a production-ready Streamlit application that brings together a well-structured ML backend and a user-friendly multi-page frontend. It processes 44 features spanning agricultural productivity, socio-economic indicators, weather patterns, and geographic context to produce an accurate estimate of annual farmer income. The trained Gradient Boosting pipeline is persisted with joblib and loaded instantly on subsequent runs, making the app suitable for real-time inference without re-training. The model architecture, performance metrics, and feature importance are fully exposed through the Model Insights page, ensuring transparency and interpretability.
